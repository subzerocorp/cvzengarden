## Why
Marcus's four-job résumé printed with page 1 seventy percent blank, Priya's Switchyard printout lost every bullet, and Nightgarden prints secondary text in pale lilac; the reference themes must teach break discipline on a senior résumé, not just on Jordan (Marcus, Priya, Mika, Elena).
## Covers
E1, E2, E3 (pale print ink) — Marcus, Priya, Mika, Elena
## Scope in
- `themes/quarto.css`, `themes/switchyard.css`, `themes/nightgarden.css` print rules: `.rz-section { break-inside: auto }`; keep `break-inside: avoid` on `.rz-entry` and `break-after: avoid` on `.rz-section-title` / `.rz-entry-header`; remove the unconditional `break-before: page` on `.rz-section--projects` in Switchyard
- Switchyard `@media print .rz-bullet::before` gets a printed marker (ink-coloured bar or bullet with `print-color-adjust: exact` fallback to a text glyph)
- Nightgarden `@media print .rz-entry-secondary`, `.rz-location`, `.rz-dates`, `.rz-meta` colour ≥ WCAG 4.5:1 against white
- New fixture `frontend/fixtures/long-resume.json` (fictional senior engineer: 4 jobs with 4–6 highlights each, 2 volunteer, 2 education, awards, 3 projects) and `frontend/fixtures/long-resume.html` rendered by the crate
- Probe helpers: rasterise PDF page 1 and measure the lowest non-background row (fill ratio)
## Scope out
- Nightgarden name overflow and `rz-rise` (ZG-12)
- Fonts (ZG-13)
- Widow/orphan tuning beyond what exists
- Changing Quarto's Letter / Switchyard's A4 choice
## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Probe `ZG-11/page1-fill`: printing `long-resume.html` with Quarto and with Switchyard, page 1's lowest inked row is ≥ 85% of the printable height (rasterised via Playwright PDF → PNG; helper prints the ratio)
- [ ] Probe `ZG-11/page-count`: `long-resume.html` prints to ≤ 3 pages in Quarto (Letter) and ≤ 3 in Switchyard (A4); `skeleton/example.html` prints to ≤ 2 pages in Quarto and Switchyard (was 3) and ≤ 2 in Nightgarden
- [ ] Probe `ZG-11/no-forced-break`: `themes/switchyard.css` contains no `break-before: page` / `page-break-before: always`; no first-party theme has `break-inside: avoid` or `avoid-page` on `.rz-section`
- [ ] Probe `ZG-11/entry-intact`: in the Quarto PDF of `long-resume.html`, no `.rz-entry-header` is split across pages (each `h3` and its `.rz-dates` render on the same page — check via print-emulated `getBoundingClientRect` against page height)
- [ ] Probe `ZG-11/bullets-print`: with Switchyard in print emulation, `getComputedStyle(bullet, '::before').content` is not `none`/`""` **or** `list-style-type` is not `none`, and the rasterised page shows ink left of the first bullet's text box (pixel probe); Quarto and Nightgarden unchanged
- [ ] Probe `ZG-11/print-ink`: in print emulation, Nightgarden `.rz-entry-secondary` computed colour contrast against `#fff` ≥ 4.5 (helper `contrastRatio`)
- [ ] BAR-T1 still holds (Quarto/Switchyard no keyframes; Nightgarden has keyframes + reduced-motion); BAR-U3 Nightgarden print ≤ 2 pages
- [ ] `just verify` green
