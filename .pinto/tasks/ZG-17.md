## Scope in
- Root `LICENSE` = the standard MIT text (`Permission is hereby granted, free of charge…`) with a `Copyright (c) <year> <holder>` line; `renderer/Cargo.toml` `license` aligned to `MIT`
- `themes/README.md` "License" section (`## License` up to the next `## ` heading): accepted licenses for submitted Themes are **MIT** or **CC BY 4.0**; the Garden may host them on free and paid pages; attribution (name + link, from the `Author:` / `URL:` header lines) is displayed on the theme's card in the switcher (ZG-16); non-commercial licenses (CC BY-NC-*) are not accepted because job seekers will pay for hosted pages
- `_blank.css` `License:` line lists exactly those two options (`MIT` and `CC BY 4.0`) — today it reads `(e.g. CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, MIT)`, Mika's "a menu, not a policy"
- `README.md`: a "License" line naming MIT and the sentence "Your résumé JSON stays yours" (ownership)
- A static probe `ZG-17/license-docs` in `frontend/scripts/probes.mjs` (file reads, no browser) that holds the greps below, so ZG-18 (CONTRIBUTING pointer) and ZG-19 (README / `_blank.css` edits) cannot silently reintroduce a hosted-page promise or a non-commercial example
## Scope out
- Attribution on hosted/published pages — no round-1 PBI renders a Designer credit on `/r/{id}` (ZG-21 serves a bare Skeleton plus the theme `<link>`), so the terms must not promise one. A hosted-page byline is a separate PBI against ZG-21 if the human wants it

- Trademark policy for the name ResumeZen
- Privacy policy text (ZG-22)
