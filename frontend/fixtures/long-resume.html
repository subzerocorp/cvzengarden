Summary

Principal engineer with fourteen years across payments, logistics, and developer platforms. I lead teams that ship boring, durable systems: event-sourced ledgers that reconcile to the cent, schedulers that survive a region outage, and build pipelines that stay green for years. I write the design docs first, argue for the smallest viable architecture, and stay close enough to the code to review every migration.

I care most about the people who inherit what we build. That means readable service boundaries, on-call rotations that let engineers sleep, and interfaces documented well enough that a new hire can ship a change in their first week. Outside of work I mentor early-career engineers through a Chicago nonprofit and maintain two small open-source libraries.
